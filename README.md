# Case Competition Portfolio

A collection of business case competition projects demonstrating structured problem solving, market research, product strategy, business analysis, and presentation design. These projects were developed to solve real-world business challenges through data-driven insights and innovative solutions.

## Projects

### 1. LockFlow Packaging Innovation
Developed an innovative packaging solution to address the "last 10%" product wastage problem in personal care bottles. The proposed LockFlow system combines an airless piston, flexible wiper ring, and ratchet-lock mechanism to achieve near-total product utilization while improving customer experience, sustainability, and brand value.

**Highlights**
- Product Innovation
- Consumer Insights
- Unit Economics
- Business Strategy
- Sustainability Analysis
- Go-to-Market Recommendations
